## Coverage caveats — read before treating this as complete

- **196** End-of-Life bulletins contributed **1448** part numbers that appear in NO current datasheet — discontinued hardware the datasheet sweep alone cannot see.
- **2** datasheets could not be read (fetch error or robots).
- **273** datasheets were read but contained no ordering table with a
  part-number column, so they contributed nothing. Some product pages carry their
  ordering information in a PDF, which this pass does not read.
- This covers products Cisco currently publishes a datasheet for. Discontinued part
  numbers whose datasheet has been retired are reachable through the End-of-Life
  bulletins instead, which is a separate source.
- Software, licence and service SKUs are included where they appear in an ordering
  table. They are part numbers, but they are not hardware.
