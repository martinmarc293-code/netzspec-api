## Why T1/T2 yield nothing (the real extractor, `extract_document`, run on the cached page)

1. `MODEL_HDR = ^(model|sku|part number|product number|product id)$` is anchored: "Switch model" is not a model column.
2. Even with "Switch model" admitted, the page's PID list is EMPTY (`pids: []`, counts A 0 / B 42 / C 0): `HW_PID` does not
   recognise `CGP-*`, so a model-column table of CGP rows attributes nothing, and records no defect (a silent drop).

## Question for the ruling (parked; nothing built)

pon_ports is published, so it stays required, but filling it is extractor work in three parts that land together: (a)
MODEL_HDR admits "switch model(s)" only; (b) `CGP-*` recognised as hardware PIDs, and a model-column row with no attributable
PID recorded as a defect instead of dropped; (c) the two alias rules ahead of ports/uplink_ports. Then one apply of e947e1ba.
The 2960 PoE loss under (a) needs its own answer (keep those tables on their current path, or accept the loss).
pon_standard: no label states it as its own attribute; it is stated inside the port cell ("8 GPON ports"), in the family
Standards row (G.984.x = GPON) and in the description. It is PUBLISHED, not absent, so demoting it would delete closable
work. Options: a strict companion parse of the port cell (one label, two cups: no such mechanism exists today), or a
registered derivation from the G.984/G.987/G.9807/G.989 recommendation numbers. Recommendation: the derivation.
